SUMMARY OF CLOUD COMPUTING MODELS:
1. Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS): Provision compute, storage, networks.
2. Platform as a Service (PaaS): Manage applications without managing infrastructure.
3. Software as a Service (SaaS): Access web-based applications directly.

KEY BENEFIT:
Scalability, Cost Efficiency, and Reliability.
